Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Summary

Vocab does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no analytics tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Vocab does not collect your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Where your data lives

Words you look up, words you save, your tags, and your review history are stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. We operate no account system and no user database, so there is no copy of your data on any server we control. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Dictionary content and network requests

When you look up a word, the app sends that single word to our dictionary sources (Wiktionary and, where available, the Free Dictionary API) to retrieve its definition. Only the word you typed is transmitted. We do not send your saved list, tags, review history, or any identifier. Dictionary providers may log requests under their own privacy policies.

Lookups you have already made are cached on your device so they can be shown to you offline.

In-app purchases

The paid upgrade is purchased and processed entirely by Google Play. Vocab never sees or receives your payment details. Google provides us with only anonymous purchase confirmations, handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Vocab requests no runtime permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, files, contacts, or location. It requests internet access solely to retrieve dictionary definitions and to reach Google Play Billing.

Children

Vocab is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete. If you wish to remove everything the app knows, uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's public source repository.
