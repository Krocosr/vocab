## Notes on running the test well

- **Say the 14-day ask up front, every time.** The single most common failure is
  people installing, liking it, and uninstalling in week one. You lose the whole
  window.
- **Ask for behaviour, not stars.** A tester who tells you the search is confusing
  is worth ten one-star ratings. Tell people you don't want a rating.
- **Do not pay for reviews or ratings.** That is the line that gets an account
  permanently actioned, and it voids the effort of everything else here.
- **Over-recruit.** Aim for 15–18 so two people dropping out doesn't cost you the
  gate.
- **Keep a rolling note** of what testers said. Google asks you to summarise this
  in the production-access form, and real specifics read as genuine engagement.
- During the 14 days you can push new builds to the closed track; use the
  feedback to fix real bugs before applying.
